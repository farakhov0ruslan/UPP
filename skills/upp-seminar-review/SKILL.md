# UPP Seminar Review

Use when checking whether a seminar answer is complete, defensible, and ready to export.

## Review procedure

1. Determine the active `СXX` from the user's request and the team workbook. Prove that the matching lecture and seminar/card files exist and read them before judging the sheet.
2. Read the case, assessment rules, glossary, and all relevant earlier seminar sheets in the same team workbook. If a required source is missing, report the gap instead of guessing.
3. Compare the sheet with the task's required result, not with a guessed ideal answer. Preserve the workbook's layout and distinguish yellow input cells from gray calculated cells.
4. Check every conclusion for a source or fact code. Mark unsupported causes as hypotheses and leave unavailable data explicit.
5. Check that the person preparing a decision is separated from the person with final authority. Use the case catalogue `P01–P06`; do not grant RП, владельцу продукта, or a study-team role powers they do not have.
6. Check that alternatives include consequences, evidence, limits, timing, and the next verifiable action. For conditional exercises, do not present the condition as an event that already happened.
7. Build a traceability note from prior answer → current requirement → current cell/range → later consequence. Report contradictions separately from optional improvements.

## S02 minimum

For `С02_Решения`, expect three blocks: a concise map of six rights, a detailed date-change decision, and a detailed pilot-admission decision. The date block must keep the approved base date unchanged until the curator decides. The admission block must keep operations as the final `P04` authority and treat the `P05` information-security decision separately. Missing recovery evidence blocks a final admission recommendation.

Do not require a large influence matrix or six extra detailed appeals when the S02 card does not ask for them.

## Reporting

Return findings as `must fix`, `should verify`, and `already satisfies`. Cite the sheet and row/range, list the lecture/seminar files verified, and state which previous answers were carried forward. Do not overwrite answers, fabricate evidence, or make an unapproved management choice.
